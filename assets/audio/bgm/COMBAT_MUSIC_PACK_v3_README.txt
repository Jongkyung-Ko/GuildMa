GuildMaster Combat BGM Pack v3
=================================

Four multi-track combat arrangements. Each track is a 32-bar loop in 4/4.

03_combat_epic_rock.mid
- 142 BPM. Energetic epic rock.
- Rhythm + lead electric guitars, picked bass, orchestral brass, cinematic strings, synth choir, full rock drums.

04_combat_metal_drive.mid
- 158 BPM. Fast, aggressive metal drive.
- Palm-muted guitar pulse, lead guitar, driving bass, brass accents, string ostinato, synth atmosphere, hard drums.

05_combat_heroic_anthem.mid
- 136 BPM. Bright heroic action theme.
- Electric guitars, bass, orchestral brass, moving strings, atmospheric synth, punchy rock drums.

06_combat_boss_intensity.mid
- 164 BPM. High-intensity boss battle.
- Heavy guitars, urgent bass, dramatic brass, dark strings, synth choir, hard-hitting drums and crash accents.

All files are Standard MIDI Format 1 with separate instrument tracks and General MIDI programs.
MIDI contains note/instrument instructions, not recorded audio. GuildMaster Godot 4.3 currently plays OGG renders,
not raw MIDI. Render these files offline with the project's FluidSynth/SoundFont pipeline to loopable OGG files,
then register the OGGs in AudioManager.

Suggested combat playlist:
- Normal encounters: Epic Rock -> Heroic Anthem -> Metal Drive -> repeat/shuffle without immediate repeats.
- Boss encounters: Boss Intensity (or a weighted boss playlist).
- Switch tracks only at a bar/loop boundary or use a short crossfade; do not abruptly stop the current track.
